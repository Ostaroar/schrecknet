SchreckNet · Design direction · R1 · 2026-07-18

Old blood, new interface.

A rebuild of VDB for Vampire: The Eternal Struggle, hosting the V5 format exclusively — every existing feature, re-set in a dark-first, keyboard-first design. The whole card database lives in your browser (SQLite + WASM), so every screen below works offline and reacts instantly. Palatino-family serifs carry the old-world voice; the UI around them stays quiet, modern, and fast.

Design tokens

The app commits to a dark ground — a plum-black biased toward the accent, never neutral grey. Blood-crimson is spent only on primary actions, active filters and data marks; gold is reserved for superior disciplines and card lore. A light app theme ships too (same tokens, re-stepped), but dark is the definitive reading.

01Crypt search

All twenty-plus vdb filter families, reorganized into a scannable rail: the ones you touch constantly (text, disciplines, capacity, clan, group) sit open; title, traits, set, precon and artist collapse. Results are a dense, sortable table — hover any row to preview the card and add it to the active deck.
